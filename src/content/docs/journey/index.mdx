This is the map. You start at "I have used a chatbot once". You finish able to design a system of several assistants for a job you have never seen, and to explain each choice. Each level adds at most three ideas. Every idea comes back in later levels, so nothing is learned once and dropped.

## The running task

One job grows with you: the monthly money admin. It starts as Sam splitting flat-share bills with two flatmates, Priya and Tom. It grows into Sam doing the month-end for Mina, who owns a small shop. By level 8 you can break up a new job without help.

Along the way the assistant makes the same slip at every level: it adds February twice. Each level gives you a better way to deal with it. By level 5 you will spot it yourself.

## How each chapter works

Every chapter has the same nine parts, in the same order, so you always know where you are: Scene, The idea, Try it, Under the bonnet, Expert corner, Agent note, Mistakes people make, Checkpoint and Bridge. Try it takes about five minutes with any chat tool and costs nothing. The checkpoint ends with three questions and answers, so you can tell whether to move on.
