This is the map. You start at "I have used a chatbot once". You finish able to design a checked workflow for a task you know well, and to explain each choice. Each level adds three to five ideas, and the chapter says which ones matter most. Every idea comes back in later levels, so nothing is learned once and dropped.

If you already use an assistant and want repeatable results, take the [consistency fast track](../start-here/consistency-fast-track/). It is eight steps.

New here? Start with the [five-minute win](./00-hello-robot/#five-minute-win).

## The running task

One job grows with you: the monthly money admin. It starts as Sam splitting flat-share bills with two flatmates, Priya and Tom. It grows into Sam preparing the month-end for Mina, who owns a small candle shop. By level 8 you can break up a new job without help.

Along the way the same slip comes back: a month counted twice, or a month missing, and the total comes out wrong. Each level gives you a better way to deal with it. By level 5 you will spot it yourself.

## How long it takes

Reading times are counted at 200 words a minute over the chapter text, rounded up to the nearest 5 minutes. Code blocks, Go deeper blocks, the Agent note and Try it are not counted. The Try it time counts each new chat, paste, read, compare, count, hand-add and one-line write as one step, and each spreadsheet step as two, rounded up to the next 5 minutes. Level 8 is your own design work, so it has a step count and no minutes. Reading and Try it add up to the time for a level.

| Level | Reading | Try it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 10 minutes | 20 minutes (the five-minute win is 5 of them) |
| 1 | 10 minutes | 30 minutes (the four passes alone are 10) |
| 2 | 10 minutes | 15 minutes |
| 3 | 10 minutes | 15 minutes |

| 7 | 10 minutes | 15 minutes |
| 8 | 10 minutes | about 7 steps, no time given |

## How each chapter works

Every chapter shows three boxes for three readers: New to this, Want consistency and At work. Take the ones that are yours. Expert corner, Agent note and the folded Go deeper blocks are optional extras. Each chapter ends with three questions and answers, so you can tell whether to move on.

New to this? Read [how to read this guide](../start-here/how-to-read/) first. Unsure of a word? Look it up in the [glossary](../glossary/). Want to tick off what you can now do? Use the [progress page](./progress/).
