## From the trenches: why this guide exists

I am Glen Keane. I build production software and distributed systems (formerly Wayflyer and Nearform), and currently ship automated insurance systems and AI tooling at Brightbeam.

Most AI advice stops at the demo. In production, things fall apart. When you rely on AI assistants for serious work, you hit the **80% calculator trap**: an adding machine that calculates correctly 4 times out of 5 does not automate your work; it doubles your anxiety. If you have to babysit every word or line of output, you suffer review fatigue and end up rubber-stamping results you do not understand. That is how comprehension debt explodes.

Getting reliable output is not about secret prompt incantations or expensive SaaS tools. It comes from disciplined habits: specifying intent like a Product Manager, catching mistakes with automatic test gates, and building disposable micro-tools that solve one friction point at a time. This guide, the Harness AI guide, teaches how.

## What the tests showed: a €133 bill counted twice

I gave an assistant 40 monthly bill amounts for 39 months and asked it to split the total three ways. In 20 of 20 isolated runs, the reply noticed that the count was one too many. In 20 of 20, it still led with a total that counts €133 twice. A reader who stops at the first line pays the wrong share.
