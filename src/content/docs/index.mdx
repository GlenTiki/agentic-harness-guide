*For anyone who has used a chat assistant at least once and knows a reply can be wrong, arriving from a link with one screen of patience. You leave with a place to start; without it, you begin at a level too easy or too hard and give up.*

Start with the five-minute win: paste a list of 13 figures for 12 months into your assistant and compare its total with yours. One assistant I tested, claude-sonnet-5-5, gave the right total, 2,120.00, in 20 of 20 recorded trials of that prompt. A longer list went worse: 40 amounts for 39 months, one pasted twice, gave the wrong total 7,414 against the right 7,281 in 20 of 20 trials; every reply flagged "40 amounts for 39 months" and none found the repeat. A three-line check finds it, and the nine levels teach you to ask for checks like that rather than trust the tone. The [evidence page](./evidence/) has the counts.

## What is an agentic harness?

An agentic harness is the set of limits and checks around an AI assistant that takes steps for you, such as reading a file or drafting a message. It makes the assistant's work dependable and checkable. This guide teaches you to build one, from your first chat to a team of assistants. When the same slip comes back, you work through the [weak-spot loop](./method/engineer-weak-spots/#the-loop): notice it, name it, engineer a fix, prove the fix can fail, keep it.

## Start here

1. **Do the five-minute win.** [Level 0](./journey/00-hello-robot/#five-minute-win) shows how to catch a repeated figure before it becomes a wrong total.
2. **Pick your route.** [Choose a route](./start-here/how-to-read/) by how you use an assistant: at work, in a chat window, or as an engineer.
3. **Keep one prompt.** [Refiner lite](./appendix/prompt-refiner/#refiner-lite) tightens a vague ask. Paste it into any chat assistant.

You do not need the words "agentic" or "harness" to start. [Engineers: start with the engineer route.](./start-here/engineer-route/)
