Treating an AI assistant as a faster keyboard for chat pair-programming is a category error. If you babysit every token, you suffer review fatigue and end up rubber-stamping code you do not understand.

The compounding breakthrough happens when you step out of the editor and become the **Product Manager of your own day**. The assistant acts as your autonomous Tech Lead: it drafts micro-tools in isolated workspaces, proves them with mechanical test gates, and locks them into your toolbelt. When a friction point repeats, use the [weak-spot loop](./method/engineer-weak-spots/#the-loop): notice it, name it, engineer a fix, prove the fix can fail, keep it.
