"Agentic" means an assistant that takes steps for you, such as reading a file or drafting a message. A "harness" is the set of limits and checks around it. You do not need either word to start.

Assistants write confident answers that can be wrong. This guide shows you how to catch that. Here is the kind of slip it teaches you to catch. You paste a year of shared bills, and February is in the list twice by accident.

```text
Example of a WRONG reply, shown for practice:
Total for the year: 2,318.00. Each of the three of us pays 772.67.

A checked reply:
I counted 13 figures for 12 months. February appears twice.
Total without the repeat: 2,120.00. Shares: 706.67, 706.67 and 706.66.
```

The wrong reply sounds sure and costs each person 66.00 too much. The checked reply says what it counted. Nine levels teach you to ask for that.
