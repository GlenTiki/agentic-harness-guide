If you already practise a profession, start from this: the assistant is a fast, fallible junior. It drafts quickly and it makes mistakes. Its output stays yours. This route keeps the pages that help with that and skips the ones that do not.

No page here replaces your organisation's AI policy. Read the policy first. If it forbids something this guide shows, the policy wins.

## The route, in order

1. [Level 0: Hello, robot](../../journey/00-hello-robot/). Skip the Scene if you wish. Start the Try it at [the five-minute win](../../journey/00-hello-robot/#five-minute-win), the money question. It shows a total you must check yourself.
2. [Level 1: Say it properly](../../journey/01-say-it-properly/). Bring your own standard. If you have a style guide, a policy or a checklist, paste it as the constraints.
3. [Level 2: House rules](../../journey/02-house-rules/). Turn your standard into standing rules, then seed a violation and confirm the assistant flags it. The optional work part at the end of the Try it walks through one.
4. [Using an assistant at work](../../journey/professional-guardrails/). Confidentiality, policy, sign-off, a fallback reviewer for solo work, what never to delegate, sampling, what to do when it goes wrong and the working paper.
5. [Why it sounds sure](../../journey/why-it-sounds-sure/). Why an assistant sounds certain when it is wrong, with a four-prompt test.
6. [Level 5: Check the work](../../journey/05-check-the-work/). Write a checkable done, and use a reviewer who is not the preparer, or the fallback reviewer if you work alone.
7. [Spreadsheet Monday](../../workflows/spreadsheet-monday/). Let the assistant write the formula, then check the formula. Never ask it to add in prose.
8. [Small shop month-end](../../workflows/small-shop-month-end/). A worked month-end: reconciliation, cut-off, variance and sign-off.
9. [Review content](../../workflows/review-content/). Review writing against a brief.
10. [Research a decision](../../workflows/research-a-decision/). Sources, a comparison and a stated recommendation.
11. Levels [3](../../journey/03-give-it-hands/) and [4](../../journey/04-trust-but-verify/), when you connect systems. Read the approval fatigue paragraph in level 4 even if you never connect anything. A human reviewer who approves too much stops reading too. The [glossary](../../glossary/#approval-fatigue) defines it.

## Which boxes to read

Each chapter has several boxes. Read At work, Expert corner and Mistakes people make. You can ignore the Agent note and New to this. Agent notes are rules for software that reads the page.

## What to skip

On the reference shelf, skip hooks, worktree isolation, small pull requests, spec-driven development and test-first and gates. They are about software delivery. The ideas behind them, such as a separate reviewer and a written definition of done, are already in level 5.
