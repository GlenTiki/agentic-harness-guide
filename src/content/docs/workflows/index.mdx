---
title: Workflows
description: Seven worked cases that apply the journey's ideas to real jobs.
---

Each workflow takes one real job and walks it through a refinement loop. Read the matching level first.

- [Improve code](./improve-code/): Refine a piece of code in small, tested passes with an independent review. Pairs with level 5.
- [Improve a design](./improve-design/): Refine a layout or visual design against a written brief and a checklist. Pairs with level 6.
- [Build a budget](./build-a-budget/): Build a household or event budget with the assistant, and check the totals. Pairs with level 3.
- [Connect systems](./connect-systems/): Decide what to connect, in what order, with what access. Pairs with levels 3 and 4.
- [Review content](./review-content/): Review writing against a brief with a reviewer who sees only the draft. Pairs with level 5.
- [Define a workflow](./define-a-workflow/): Turn a repeated job into a written workflow with steps, checks and stop points. Pairs with level 8.
- [Research a decision](./research-a-decision/): Research a choice with sources, a comparison table and a stated recommendation. Pairs with level 6.
