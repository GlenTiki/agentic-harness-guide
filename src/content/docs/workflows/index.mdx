title: Workflows
description: Ten worked jobs that apply the journey's ideas, each tagged with what you need to try it.
---

Each workflow takes one real job and walks it through a refinement loop. Read the matching level first. The tag says what you need.

1. [Build a budget](./build-a-budget/): Build a budget for a small shop with the assistant, and check the totals. Pairs with levels 1 and 5. *Chat only (a spreadsheet helps).*
2. [Spreadsheet Monday](./spreadsheet-monday/): Use the assistant to write formulas you can check, instead of adding in prose. Pairs with level 5. *Chat and a spreadsheet.*
3. [Review content](./review-content/): Review writing against a brief with a reviewer who sees only the draft. Pairs with level 5. *Chat only.*
4. [Improve code](./improve-code/): Refine a piece of code in small, tested passes with an independent review. Pairs with level 5. *Needs code.*
5. [Improve a design](./improve-design/): Refine a layout or visual design against a written brief and a checklist. Pairs with level 5. *Chat only to start; needs five real people for the last step.*
6. [Connect systems](./connect-systems/): Decide what to connect, in what order, with what access. Pairs with levels 3 and 4. *Needs connected tools.*
7. [Small shop month-end](./small-shop-month-end/): Work through a credible September month-end, from bank reconciliation to sign-off. Pairs with levels 5 and 7. *Chat and a spreadsheet.*
8. [Define a workflow](./define-a-workflow/): Turn a repeated job into a written workflow with steps, checks and stop points. Pairs with level 8. *Chat only.*
9. [Research a decision](./research-a-decision/): Research a choice with sources, a comparison table and a stated recommendation. Pairs with level 8. *Chat only.*
10. [Design reps](./design-reps/): Practise the six-step method on four more jobs, one with pen and paper. Pairs with level 8. *Chat only, plus pen and paper.*
