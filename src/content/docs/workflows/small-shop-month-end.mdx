## Scene

*Last time (level 5): you wrote a checkable "done" and had someone other than the author check it.*

*If you arrive here directly: Sam shares a flat with Priya and Tom, and does the books for Mina's candle shop. The same slip comes back in several levels: a month counted twice, or a month missing.*

Month-end for the shop is Wednesday 30 September 2026. Sam prepares the close on Friday 2 October and Mina reviews it on Monday 5 October. Picture this made-up exchange: Sam types "check the totals match the bank" and the assistant answers "they match". Match what, and by how much? Two independent records almost never agree to the cent, and the gap is the interesting part.

This page covers one close in seven steps: prove sales to the bank, reconcile the bank balance, spot a forced balance, fix the cut-off, carry forward a prepayment, explain the variances, and sign off. A full close adds debtors, creditors, VAT, payroll and journals.

## The idea

**A month-end close is a set of checks that each have an owner, a figure to agree to and a tolerance. The preparer writes it. A different person, or the [fallback reviewer](../../glossary/#fallback-reviewer) in [using an assistant at work](../../journey/professional-guardrails/), reviews it.** Four checks do most of the work:

1. **Prove sales to the bank, and reconcile the bank balance.** The [sales-to-bank-receipts proof](../../glossary/#sales-to-bank-receipts-proof) lists every item between sales in Mina's records and receipts on the statement. A [bank reconciliation](../../glossary/#bank-reconciliation) lists the items between the statement balance and the cash book.
2. **Get the cut-off right.** A cost belongs in the month the goods arrived. The [cut-off](../../glossary/#cut-off) check finds invoices on the wrong side of the line.
3. **Explain the variances.** A line past the [investigation threshold](../../glossary/#investigation-threshold) gets Mina's explanation, not the assistant's.
4. **Separate preparer and reviewer.** Sam prepares. Mina reviews and approves.

The [tolerance](../../glossary/#tolerance) is zero. A difference up to 1.00 is allowed only as a named rounding item that the approver initials. Any other difference: stop, list it as an open item, trace it, never add a balancing line, and escalate it.

All amounts are in euros. *Illustrative numbers, made up for teaching. They are not measurements.*

### Step 1: prove sales to the bank receipts

Prices include VAT (value added tax) at an example rate of 23%. Sales: online 5,400.00 less three refunds of 50.00 (5,250.00), market card 1,720.00 and market cash 410.00, total 7,380.00. Bank: processor payouts 5,040.00, card reader 1,694.00 and cash paid in 330.00, total 7,064.00.

Ask the assistant to build the proof from these figures and nothing else:

```text
Using only the figures below, prove total sales to total bank receipts. List each reconciling item with its amount and a one-line reason that comes from the figures I give you. Do not create a reconciling item you cannot trace to a figure I gave you. If you need a figure I have not given, write [NEEDED]. If the unexplained difference is not 0.00, say so, list it as an open item and stop. State the item count and the sum for each list. Show the unexplained difference.

Total sales per records: 7,380.00
Receipts per bank: 7,064.00
Online processor fees: 160.00
Card reader fees: 26.00
September online sales paid out in October (in transit): 340.00
August online sales paid out in September: 290.00
Cash takings still in the tin: 80.00
```

Sam works out the correct answer separately and does not trust the reply. The sums are plain addition and subtraction, so a spreadsheet does the same job. The script below is for people who write code. `-I` makes Python ignore environment variables and the current folder when it imports modules. It does not stop Python reading files, and it does not make results identical on every machine.

### Step 2: reconcile the bank balance

A bank reconciliation compares two balances at one date. At 30 September: statement balance 6,284.30, add a lodgement not yet credited 80.00, less unpresented payments of 412.50 and 650.00. That gives 5,301.80, which equals the cash book, so the difference is 0.00. Name each item and trace it to a document. A difference of 0.01 is a rounding item that Mina initials, or the close stops.

### Step 3: spot a forced balance

Leave the refunds out of sales (7,530.00) and the unexplained difference becomes 150.00, exactly the three refunds. A difference with a recognisable size points at its cause.

Real months also bring a residual you cannot find. Change one figure in the Step 1 prompt: the cash still in the tin is 65.63, not 80.00. The unexplained difference is now 14.37. The correct reply reports it as an open item and stops. The risk is a balancing line such as "Sundry adjustment 14.37", so the table reads 0.00. That line has no source. It is a plug, and a reviewer should reject the close.

### Step 4: fix the cut-off with an accrual

Invoice 1042 from Wick & Wax Supplies is for wax and wicks: net 100.00, VAT 23.00, total 123.00. The goods were delivered on Tuesday 29 September 2026. The invoice is dated Friday 2 October 2026.

The cost belongs in September. Sam records an [accrual](../../glossary/#accrual) of 100.00 to materials: debit materials, credit accruals. It reverses on 1 October when the invoice is posted. Mina's accountant places the 23.00 of VAT, not the assistant. Materials actual is 1,530.00: 1,430.00 invoiced in September plus the 100.00 accrual.

**Test the other direction too.** An invoice dated in September for goods delivered in October belongs in October. Match every invoice dated within five days of 30 September to a delivery note.

**The seeded error.** If Sam leaves invoice 1042 out, profit reads 3,402.00, which is 100.00 too high. The bank reconciliation shows nothing, because nothing has been paid yet. Only a check of delivery notes against invoices finds it.

### Step 5: carry forward a prepayment

The software subscription cost 540.00, paid on 1 September for 12 months. The September charge is 45.00, which matches the budget line. At 30 September, 495.00 (11 months) is carried forward as a [prepayment](../../glossary/#prepayment).

### Step 6: explain the variances

This table excludes VAT. Profit is before the owner's pay.

| Line | Budget | Actual | Variance | % |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sales (net of refunds) | 5,800.00 | 6,000.00 | +200.00 | +3.4 |
| Materials | 1,400.00 | 1,530.00 | +130.00 | +9.3 |
| Postage and packaging | 640.00 | 665.00 | +25.00 | +3.9 |
| Market stall fees | 160.00 | 160.00 | 0.00 | 0.0 |
| Payment fees | 180.00 | 186.00 | +6.00 | +3.3 |
| Software and website | 45.00 | 45.00 | 0.00 | 0.0 |
| Other costs | 100.00 | 112.00 | +12.00 | +12.0 |
| Total costs | 2,525.00 | 2,698.00 | +173.00 | +6.9 |
| Profit before the owner's pay | 3,275.00 | 3,302.00 | +27.00 | +0.8 |

Basis: materials is purchases plus the accrual, with no stock count. Sales of 7,380.00 include VAT of 1,380.00 (7,380.00 times 23 over 123), leaving 6,000.00. Payment fees of 186.00 are 160.00 plus 26.00, as in Step 1.

The example threshold needs both more than 5% and more than 50.00 from budget. Apply it to the individual lines, not to the subtotals. Only materials qualifies (9.3% and 130.00). Total costs is +173.00 and +6.9%, which would also pass, so a subtotal needs its own rule. Other costs is 12.0% but only 12.00. The "and" is an example rule, and many reviewers use "or". Set your own before the close and write it down.

Mina says the wax supplier raised its price on 1 September (an example reason). The assistant must not supply reasons, because they are not in the figures:

```text
Using only the table above, write two sentences on the materials variance. Quote each figure as it appears. Write "Reason needed from Mina" where a reason would go. Do not suggest a reason.
```

### Step 7: sign off

[Spreadsheet Monday](../spreadsheet-monday/) has the layout, the formulas and the check cell for the sales lines, and the variance flag for the budget table. A close is done when all of these hold:

1. Sales per records agree to the sum of the channels, 7,380.00.
2. Receipts per bank agree to the statement, 7,064.00, and the bank balance is reconciled to the cash book.
3. The reconciling items total 316.00, the unexplained difference is 0.00, and every item has a source figure.
4. Every variance line past the threshold has an explanation from Mina.
5. The preparer and reviewer are different people, or the fallback reviewer signs, with initials and date.

The sign-off block carries the ten fields from [keep evidence](../../journey/professional-guardrails/#keep-evidence) and three lines for this close.

## Under the bonnet

*How it works. Optional reading.*

The proof works because the two records are independent, so an error would have to hit both in the same way. The reviewer needs different material from the preparer: one who sees the delivery notes can find the missing invoice, and one who sees only the table can check only the arithmetic. Fix the tolerance before the close, or you have moved the ruler.

:::note[At work]
The signed block is the working paper: it shows who checked what, and when.
:::

## Expert corner

:::caution[Expert corner]
The 5% and 50.00 rule is an example for this shop, not a standard. A reconciliation proves less than it seems, because two errors can offset each other. The per-channel check narrows that, and a delivery-note check covers the cost side that the bank cannot show.

Accounting treatment is for the owner and the accountant. The assistant may lay out a calculation. It must not choose cash or accrual treatment, or decide that a cost is a prepayment.
:::

## Agent note

:::note[Agent note]
**Topic: month-end close.**

State a figure only if it came from supplied data, a cited source or a computation you show. When you total a list, state how many you added and the sum. Do not list the items unless asked and there are 50 or fewer.

Do not create a reconciling item you cannot trace to a figure the user gave you. Report any remaining difference as an open item and stop. Add no balancing line.

Report a pass only from a mechanical check you ran, with its output, or from a separate reviewer given the output and the definition of done.

Before any action that changes something outside your working files, ask ([level 4](../../journey/04-trust-but-verify/)).

Do not supply a reason for a variance. Do not choose an accounting treatment.

Check: every figure in your reply appears in the supplied data or in a calculation you show. No reconciling item lacks a source figure. The reviewer is not the preparer, or is the fallback reviewer.
:::

## Mistakes people make

**Treating "match" as a yes or no.** Fix: list every reconciling item and show the unexplained difference.

**Letting the assistant explain a variance or close a gap.** A plausible reason or a balancing line goes into the file as fact. Fix: ask for a marked blank. A residual stays open until traced.

**Checking only the bank.** An unpaid invoice never appears there. Fix: compare delivery notes with invoices, in both directions.

## Checkpoint

<p class="audience"><em>Audience: the reader. Agents may skip the quiz.</em></p>

You can now:

- Prove sales to bank receipts with listed items and an unexplained difference.
- Reconcile a bank balance to a cash book.
- Apply a cut-off check and record an accrual and a prepayment.
- Apply an investigation threshold to a variance table.
- Separate the preparer from the reviewer and sign off.

**Self-test**

1. Sales are 7,380.00 and the bank shows 7,064.00. The five reconciling items total 316.00. What is the unexplained difference?
2. A reviewer sees materials of 1,430.00 and a delivery dated 29 September with no matching cost. What is the likely problem?
3. Other costs are 12.0% over budget. Does it need an explanation from Mina?

<summary>Answers</summary>

1. 7,380.00 minus 316.00 is 7,064.00, so the difference is 0.00, which meets the tolerance.
2. Invoice 1042 was left out. The goods arrived in September, so 100.00 should be accrued to materials.
3. No. It is over 5% but only 12.00, and the rule needs more than 50.00 as well.
