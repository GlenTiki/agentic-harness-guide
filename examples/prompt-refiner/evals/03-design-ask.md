# Eval 3: a design ask

## Input

```text
Make our signup page look more professional.
```

## A good refinement must

- Not produce a design, a colour palette or copy.
- Ask who the visitors are and what "professional" means to them, each with a recommended default.
- Mark the brand rules, the page itself and the audience as [NEEDED].
- Name accessibility as a constraint to confirm, without inventing the user's standards.
- Ask for options, not one answer, or state that as an assumption.
- Give a definition of done that includes a real person testing the page.

## It fails if

- It describes a new design.
- It invents brand colours, fonts or a company name.
- It treats "professional" as self-explanatory.
