2. **One template or one email per customer?** Default: one template with merge fields (name, amount, due date), since you have no customer data yet.

## Assumptions

- This is a first reminder, not a final notice.
- The sender is a business writing to its own customers.
- The email is in English.
- The amount owed and due date differ per customer.
- The assistant should write the email only, not send it.
- A short email is wanted, with a subject line.

Output format:

## What changed

- **Added:** Context (first reminder, friendly relationship), an Inputs list, constraints on tone, length and no invented facts, an output format, a definition of done, and an "if unsure" rule.
- **Added:** a line for customers who have already paid or dispute the invoice. This is a risk the original didn't cover. Remove it if you don't want it.
- **Changed:** "the customers" became a reusable template with placeholders, since no customer data was given.
- **Left alone:** your core ask, which is a reminder email stating the amount owed and the due date.
