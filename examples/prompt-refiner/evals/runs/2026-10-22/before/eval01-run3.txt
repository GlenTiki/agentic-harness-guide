## Questions

1. **Whose budget is this?** (personal, household, small business, project/event)
   *Default: personal or household monthly budget.*
2. **What do you want from it?** (track spending, save for a goal, pay down debt, cut costs)
   *Default: get a clear picture of income vs. spending and set a savings target.*
3. **What format do you want back?** (step-by-step guide, spreadsheet layout, or a worked template)
   *Default: a spreadsheet-style template plus a short walkthrough.*

## Assumptions

- The budget is personal or household, not business.
- The period is monthly.
- You want a method and structure, not a lecture on personal finance.
- You will supply your own income and expense figures.
- No specific currency, country, or tax rules apply unless you say so.
- You are a beginner and want plain language.

## Refined prompt

```text
Goal:
Help me make a monthly budget I can actually follow. Main aim: [NEEDED: e.g. track spending, save for a goal, pay down debt].

Context:
This is for [NEEDED: personal / household / small business / project]. My experience with budgeting is [NEEDED: beginner / some / experienced]. Country and currency: [NEEDED].

Inputs:
- Monthly income after tax: [NEEDED]
- Fixed expenses (rent or mortgage, utilities, insurance, subscriptions, loan payments): [NEEDED]
- Variable expenses (food, transport, leisure, other): [NEEDED]
- Debts, with balances and interest rates, if relevant: [NEEDED]
- Savings goals, with target amounts and dates, if any: [NEEDED]
- Preferred budgeting method, if any (e.g. 50/30/20, zero-based): [NEEDED or "no preference"]

Constraints:
- Use only the figures I give you. Do not invent or assume amounts.
- Use plain language and avoid jargon.
- Do not give investment, tax, or legal advice.

Output format:
A simple budget template (table or spreadsheet-style layout) filled in with my figures, followed by a short step-by-step explanation of how to use and maintain it each month.

Definition of done:
- Income, fixed costs, variable costs, and savings are all accounted for.
- The totals balance, and any shortfall or surplus is stated clearly.
- I know what to do next month to keep it up to date.

What to do if unsure:
If any input is missing or unclear, ask me or mark it as [NEEDED]. Do not guess.
```

## What changed

- **Added:** all seven sections, which were absent. The original had only a goal. Inputs are listed as [NEEDED] placeholders because I can't know your figures.
- **Added:** constraints against inventing numbers and against giving investment, tax, or legal advice. These guard against likely risks.
- **Added:** an output format and a definition of done, so it's clear what a finished budget looks like.
- **Changed:** "Help me make a budget" became a goal with a purpose and a monthly period. The monthly period is an assumption.
- **Left alone:** your direct, casual tone and the core intent of getting help making a budget.
