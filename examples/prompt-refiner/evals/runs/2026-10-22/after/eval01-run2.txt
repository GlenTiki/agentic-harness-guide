## Questions

1. **Whose budget is it?** Personal or household, a small business, or a project or event? *Default: personal or household monthly budget.*
2. **What do you want the budget to do?** For example: track spending, save for a goal, pay off debt, or cut costs. *Default: get a clear picture of income and spending, and find room to save.*
3. **What format do you want back?** A table, a spreadsheet layout, or a step-by-step walkthrough? *Default: a table plus a short list of next steps.*

## Assumptions

- The budget is for a person or household, not a business.
- It covers one month and repeats monthly.
- You want help building the budget, so the assistant should ask for your numbers rather than assume them.
- You haven't given any income, expense, currency, or location details, so those are marked [NEEDED].
