## Questions

1. **One template or one email per customer?** Default: one template with placeholders for name, amount owed and due date, so it works for every customer.
2. **What tone?** Default: polite and professional, a friendly nudge with no threats.
3. **Should it include how to pay and who to contact?** Default: yes, as placeholders, because the right details are not in your ask.

## Assumptions

- The email goes to many customers, so it needs to be reusable.
- Each customer has a different amount and due date.
- It is a first reminder, not a final notice. I added no late fees, deadlines or consequences.
- The email is written in English.
- You are sending it on behalf of a business and will fill in the sender details.

## Refined prompt

```text
Goal:
Write a reminder email to customers who have not paid their invoice. The email must say how much they owe and when the invoice was due.

Context:
The reader is a customer with an unpaid invoice. This is a reminder, not a final notice. Sender: [NEEDED: your name and company].

Inputs:
- Customer name: [NEEDED: name, or use a placeholder such as {{customer_name}}]
- Amount owed: [NEEDED: amount and currency, or placeholder {{amount_owed}}]
- Original due date: [NEEDED: date, or placeholder {{due_date}}]
- Invoice number: [NEEDED, if you want it included]
- How to pay: [NEEDED: payment link, bank details or instructions]
- Contact for questions: [NEEDED: email or phone]

Constraints:
- Polite and professional tone. No threats, penalties or deadlines unless I provide them.
- Do not invent amounts, dates, fees, policies or payment details. Use only the inputs above.
- Keep it short.

Output format:
A subject line and an email body, ready to send once the placeholders are filled in.

Definition of done:
- The email states the amount owed and the original due date.
- It tells the customer how to pay and who to contact.
- It contains no invented facts, and every missing detail is a clearly marked placeholder.

What to do if unsure:
Ask me, or mark the gap as [NEEDED: what is missing]. Do not guess.
```

## What changed

- **Added:** the seven sections, including Context (reminder, not final notice), Inputs (name, amount, due date, payment method, contact), and the output format (subject line plus body).
- **Added:** a rule against inventing amounts, fees or deadlines, and placeholders for every missing fact.
- **Changed:** "Say how much they owe and when it was due" is now a checkable definition of done.
- **Left alone:** your goal, your wording and your plain request. I did not add detail you did not give me.
