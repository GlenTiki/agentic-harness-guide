## Questions

2. **How long and in what format?** Recommended default: a short written update of about 200 words, with a one-line verdict at the top.
3. **What counts as "late"?** Recommended default: the forecast completion date is after the committed deadline. Milestones that have slipped but don't move the final date count as "at risk", not "late".

I've used these defaults in the prompt below. Tell me if any are wrong.

## Refined prompt
```text
Goal:
Write a project update and tell me whether the project is late.

Context:
The update is for [NEEDED: audience; default assumed: project sponsor / stakeholders who are not on the team]. The project is [NEEDED: project name and one-line description]. Reporting date: [NEEDED: as-of date; default 2026-10-22].

Inputs:
- Original plan, deadline or milestone dates: [NEEDED]
- Current status: work completed, work remaining, and any changes since the plan: [NEEDED]
- Known blockers, risks or scope changes: [NEEDED]
- Any previous update to stay consistent with (optional): [NEEDED or "none"]

Constraints:
- Use only the inputs above. Do not invent dates, percentages, names or causes.
- "Late" means the forecast completion date is after the committed deadline. A slipped milestone that does not move the final date is "at risk", not "late".
- Plain language, no jargon, no padding.

Output format:
1. One-line verdict: Late / At risk / On track, with the forecast date versus the committed date.
2. A short update of about 200 words covering progress, what is left, blockers and risks, and next steps.
3. If late or at risk, one sentence on the cause and one on what would recover the date.

Definition of done:
The reader can see in the first line whether the project is late, and every claim in the update traces back to the inputs. Missing information is marked [NEEDED], not filled in.

What to do if unsure:
If an input is missing or contradictory, ask me or mark the gap with [NEEDED]. Do not guess. If there isn't enough information to judge lateness, say so and name what is missing.
```
