## Questions

1. **Is this a personal/household budget or a business/project budget?** Default: personal monthly budget.
2. **What do you want at the end: a one-off plan, or a reusable template you can track in?** Default: a monthly plan you can reuse.
3. **Is there a main goal, such as saving for something, paying off debt, or just seeing where the money goes?** Default: get a clear picture of spending and set a savings target.

## Assumptions

- The budget is personal and monthly.
- You want help building it step by step, not a generic lecture on budgeting.
- You haven't shared any income, expense or debt figures yet, so none appear below.
- Currency and location are unknown.
- The assistant may ask for your numbers one section at a time.

## Refined prompt

```text
Goal:
Help me make a budget.

Context:
This is a personal monthly budget for me [NEEDED: confirm personal vs. household vs. business]. My main aim is [NEEDED: e.g. save for something, pay down debt, track spending]. My situation: [NEEDED: living arrangement, dependents, anything else relevant]. Currency/country: [NEEDED].

Inputs:
- Monthly income after tax: [NEEDED]
- Fixed expenses (rent/mortgage, utilities, insurance, subscriptions, loan payments): [NEEDED]
- Variable expenses (food, transport, leisure, etc.): [NEEDED, rough estimates are fine]
- Debts and balances, if any: [NEEDED]
- Savings goals, with amounts and dates if any: [NEEDED]

Constraints:
- Use only the figures I give you. Do not invent or assume amounts.
- Keep it simple enough for me to maintain every month.
- Tell me which budgeting method you are using and why.
[NEEDED: any limits, e.g. tools I want to use, such as a spreadsheet or an app, or expenses that can't change]

Output format:
A budget table with income, fixed expenses, variable expenses, savings and a remaining balance. Follow it with a short list of the next steps for me.

Definition of done:
The budget adds up, covers every category I gave, and shows clearly whether I'm over or under. I know what to do next month.

What to do if unsure:
If something is missing or unclear, ask me or mark it as a gap. Do not guess numbers or make up details.
```

## What changed

- **Added:** all seven sections, because the original had only a goal. Every unknown is marked [NEEDED].
- **Added:** the inputs a budget needs (income, fixed and variable expenses, debts, goals), so the assistant doesn't have to guess.
- **Added:** an output format, a definition of done, and a "ask, don't guess" rule.
- **Changed:** "Help me make a budget" now sits under Goal in your own words.
- **Left alone:** your original wording and intent. I didn't pick a method (such as 50/30/20) for you; the prompt asks the assistant to name the one it uses.
