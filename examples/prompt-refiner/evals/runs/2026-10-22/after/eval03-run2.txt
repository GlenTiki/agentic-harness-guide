```text

This is the page new users see when they create an account. [NEEDED: product name and audience, e.g. B2B vs consumer.] "Professional" here means clean, modern and trustworthy: consistent spacing, clear visual hierarchy, tidy form styling and a polished feel.

Constraints:
- Visual and layout changes only. Do not change form fields, field names, copy, validation logic, submission behaviour or backend calls unless I say so.
- Do not add new dependencies or frameworks without asking first.
- Keep it responsive (mobile and desktop) and accessible (labels, focus states, sufficient contrast).
- Match the conventions of the existing code.
- Keep changes focused on this page. Do not restyle the rest of the site.

Output format:
- First, a short list of the problems you see in the current page and the changes you plan.
- Then make the edits.
- Finish with a summary of what you changed, by file.

Definition of done:
- The page looks consistent and polished on desktop and mobile.
- The form works exactly as before.
- Nothing outside the signup page is affected.
- Text and controls meet basic contrast and keyboard-focus requirements.

What to do if unsure:
If something is unclear, such as brand direction or whether a change would affect behaviour, ask me or mark it as an open question. Don't guess.
```
