## Questions

1. **Whose budget is this?** Personal or household, or a business or project? *Recommended default: personal monthly budget.*
2. **What is the budget for?** For example, cutting spending, saving for something, paying off debt, or just tracking. *Recommended default: get a clear picture of spending and set limits, with no specific goal.*
3. **How should the assistant get your numbers?** You can paste them, upload a statement, or answer questions one at a time. *Recommended default: it asks you questions one at a time.*

## Assumptions

- The budget is personal and monthly.
- You have no budgeting method in mind (50/30/20, zero-based, envelope), so the assistant may suggest one.
- You haven't shared any income or expense figures yet.
- You want a usable budget plan, not just general tips.
- Currency and location are unknown and left as gaps.

## Refined prompt

```text
Goal:
Help me make a budget.

Context:
This is a personal budget for [NEEDED: me alone, or a household, and how many people]. I'm [NEEDED: new to budgeting, or have tried before]. My main reason for budgeting is [NEEDED: e.g. save more, pay down debt, control spending, or just track].

Inputs:
- Monthly income after tax: [NEEDED]
- Fixed expenses (rent or mortgage, utilities, insurance, subscriptions, loan payments): [NEEDED]
- Variable expenses (food, transport, fun, etc.): [NEEDED, rough estimates are fine]
- Debts and balances, if relevant: [NEEDED]
- Savings goals and target dates, if any: [NEEDED]
- Currency and country: [NEEDED]

Constraints:
- Use only the numbers I give you. Do not invent or assume figures.
- Keep it simple enough for me to maintain every month.
- Plain language, no jargon without a short explanation.

Output format:
A budget table with categories, planned amounts and percent of income, followed by a short list of the 3 changes that would help most. Mention which budgeting method you used and why.

Definition of done:
The budget covers all my income and expenses, adds up with no unexplained gaps, matches my stated goal, and I can use it next month without further help.

What to do if unsure:
Ask me, one question at a time, for any missing input. If I can't answer, mark the gap clearly as [NEEDED] and don't guess.
```

## What changed

- **Added:** the seven sections, a list of the inputs a budget needs, a no-guessing rule, and a definition of done.
- **Changed:** nothing in your intent. "Help me make a budget" stays as the Goal.
- **Left alone:** your casual, short wording in the Goal line.
- **Why:** the original gave the assistant no income, expenses, goal or format, so it would have had to guess or reply with generic advice. Every missing fact is marked [NEEDED] rather than filled in.

## Second pass

Once you answer the three questions, I would tighten the Context and Inputs, and choose a budgeting method that fits your goal. I would also check whether the output format should be a spreadsheet layout instead of a table.
