## Questions

1. **Who reads the update?** Default: a manager or stakeholder who wants a short status, not detail.
2. **What does "late" mean here?** Default: the forecast finish date is after the committed baseline date. Say so if you mean a different milestone or a different measure.
3. **Should the update include next steps or risks?** Default: yes, briefly.

## Assumptions

- The assistant will be given project material (plan, dates, progress notes) and does not already have it.
- The update is written text of one page or less.
- "Late" is a yes/no verdict with a short reason, not a detailed schedule analysis.
- The update and the verdict go in the same reply.
- You want the verdict stated plainly, not hedged.

## Refined prompt

```text
Goal:
Write a project update, and tell me whether the project is late.

Context:
The update is for [NEEDED: audience, e.g. manager, client, steering group]. The project is [NEEDED: project name and one-line description].

Inputs:
- Baseline or committed dates: [NEEDED]
- Current progress and what is done so far: [NEEDED]
- Remaining work and current forecast finish date: [NEEDED]
- Known risks, blockers or changes in scope: [NEEDED]
- Reporting period: [NEEDED]

Constraints:
- Use only the information in Inputs. Do not invent dates, figures, names or progress.
- "Late" means the forecast finish date is after the committed baseline date, unless I say otherwise: [NEEDED: confirm or replace this definition].
- Keep it brief, one page or less, in plain language.

Output format:
1. Verdict: one line saying "Late", "On track" or "Cannot tell", with the reason in one sentence.
2. Project update: progress, what is next, risks and blockers.
Short headings and bullets are fine.

Definition of done:
- The verdict is stated first and is backed by the dates in Inputs.
- Every claim in the update comes from Inputs.
- Any missing input is listed at the end as a gap.

What to do if unsure:
If a date or fact needed for the verdict is missing, ask me for it or mark it as [NEEDED]. Do not guess. If the verdict cannot be reached without it, say "Cannot tell" and name what is missing.
```

## What changed

- **Added:** Context, Inputs, Constraints, Definition of done and What to do if unsure. The original had none of them.
- **Added:** a working definition of "late" (forecast finish after committed baseline), marked for you to confirm, because the verdict depends on it.
- **Added:** a "Cannot tell" option for the verdict, so the assistant does not guess when dates are missing.
- **Changed:** the two asks are now an ordered output, verdict first and update second.
- **Left alone:** your wording of the task, which is kept as the Goal.
- **Marked [NEEDED]:** project name, audience, dates, progress, risks and reporting period, since none were given.
