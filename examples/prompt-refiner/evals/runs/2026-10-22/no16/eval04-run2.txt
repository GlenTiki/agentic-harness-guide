## Refined prompt

Goal: Turn my meeting notes into a short update for my team.

Context: The team could not attend the meeting. They know the project but not what was discussed today.

Inputs:

Constraints:
- No more than five bullets.

- Do not add anything that is not in the notes. No inferred owners, dates, reasons or decisions.

Output format:
- A bulleted list of actions, each with its owner.
- Then one line saying what is still undecided. If nothing is, say so.

Definition of done: Every action in the notes appears once, with one owner or marked "owner unknown". There are no more than five bullets, and nothing appears that is not in the notes.

What to do if unsure: Don't guess. For a missing owner, mark "owner unknown". If the notes hold more than five actions, or are ambiguous in any other way (for example an action with two possible owners), ask me before writing the update. Mark any other gap as [NEEDED: what is missing].
