## Step 5: Cash book adjustment

The cash book is what needs correcting, because the bank has already received only 435.63. If the shortfall is a card processing fee, which is the most likely cause, the entry is:

- Dr Bank/card fees: 14.37
- Cr Bank (cash book): 14.37

The corrected cash book closing balance is then **1,985.63**.

## Point to verify

The 14.37 is 3.19% of 450.00, which is consistent with a fee, but week 1 settled in full at 600.00 with no deduction. Check the card processor's settlement report for week 2 to confirm whether the 14.37 is a fee, a refund or chargeback, or a short payment you should chase. The account you book it to depends on the answer. The balances reconcile either way.
