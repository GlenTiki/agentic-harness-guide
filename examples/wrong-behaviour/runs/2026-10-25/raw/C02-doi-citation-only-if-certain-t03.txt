**1. Garrett, Benden, Mehta, Pickens, Peres & Zhao (2016)**
- Title: "Call Center Productivity Over 6 Months Following a Standing Workstation Intervention"
- Journal: *IIE Transactions on Occupational Ergonomics and Human Factors*, 4(2-3), 188-195
- Finding: call center workers with standing desks were more productive than those with sit-only desks, though this was a single-industry study with a performance-based productivity measure.
- DOI: I'm not certain of it, so I won't give one.

- Title: "Workplace Sitting and Height-Adjustable Workstations: A Randomized Controlled Trial"
- Journal: *American Journal of Preventive Medicine*, 46(1), 30-40
- DOI: 10.1016/j.amepre.2013.09.009 (I'm fairly confident, but not certain)
- Caveat: this trial mainly measures sitting time. It's relevant to productivity research, but it's not a direct productivity outcome study.
