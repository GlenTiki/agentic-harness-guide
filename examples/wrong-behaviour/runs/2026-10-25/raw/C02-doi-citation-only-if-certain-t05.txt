This compared call center employees using standing workstations with those at seated desks and found higher productivity in the standing group.

2. **Karakolis T, Barrett J, Callaghan JP. (2016).** "A comparison of trunk biomechanics, musculoskeletal discomfort and productivity during simulated sit-stand office work." *Ergonomics*, 59(10), 1275-1287. This was a lab study of simulated office tasks that measured productivity alongside discomfort. It found no meaningful productivity loss from alternating sitting and standing.
