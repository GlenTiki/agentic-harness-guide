# September bank reconciliation

## Step 1: Check each statement foots
- Cash book: 1,000.00 + 600.00 + 450.00 - 50.00 = **2,000.00**, which matches the recorded closing balance.
- Bank: 1,000.00 + 600.00 + 435.63 - 50.00 = **1,985.63**, which matches the statement closing balance.

Neither side has an arithmetic error. The difference is a real item.

## Step 2: Compare closing balances
Cash book 2,000.00 - bank 1,985.63 = **14.37**. The cash book is higher.

## Step 3: Match item by item

| Item | Cash book | Bank | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening balance | 1,000.00 | 1,000.00 | 0.00 |
| Card sales week 1 / settlement | +600.00 | +600.00 | 0.00 |
| Card sales week 2 / settlement | +450.00 | +435.63 | **14.37** |
| Supplier payment | -50.00 | -50.00 | 0.00 |
| Closing balance | 2,000.00 | 1,985.63 | **14.37** |

Three items agree. The whole difference is in the week 2 card receipt: we recorded the gross sales of 450.00, but the bank received 435.63.

## Step 4: Classify the difference
- It is not a timing item. There are no outstanding deposits or unpresented payments, because the bank shows a week 2 settlement and it is simply smaller.
- It is most likely a **card processing or merchant fee** deducted at settlement. 14.37 is 3.19% of 450.00. This is an inference, so confirm it against the card provider's settlement report.

## Step 5: Adjust the cash book

| Entry | Dr | Cr |
|---|---|---|
| Merchant/bank fees (expense) | 14.37 | |
| Bank | | 14.37 |

## Step 6: Result

| | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cash book closing balance (as recorded) | 2,000.00 |
| Less: card fees not recorded | (14.37) |
| **Adjusted cash book balance** | **1,985.63** |
| Bank statement closing balance | 1,985.63 |
| **Unreconciled difference** | **0.00** |

The cash book and bank statement now reconcile.

## Follow-up
- The week 1 settlement was exactly 600.00 with no fee. Check whether a fee was charged elsewhere, such as on another statement or netted in a different period, or whether the provider charges fees irregularly.
- Going forward, record card receipts net of fees, or post the fee when you book the settlement, so this doesn't recur.
