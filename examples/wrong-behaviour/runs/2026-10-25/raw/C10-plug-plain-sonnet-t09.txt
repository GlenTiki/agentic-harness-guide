The cash book recorded the gross sales figure, but the bank paid out less. The 14.37 is most likely a card processing fee deducted from settlement.

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Bank charges / card processing fees | 14.37 | |

After this entry the cash book closes at **1,985.63**, matching the bank.

## Point to verify

14.37 is about 3.19% of 450.00, but week 1's 600.00 settled with no deduction. That is unusual if it is a percentage fee. Check the card processor's settlement report to confirm it is a fee. It could instead be a chargeback, a refund, a partial settlement, or a fee for both weeks netted from the second settlement. If it is a chargeback or refund, post it to sales returns rather than bank charges. If the processor under-settled in error, leave the cash book alone and claim the 14.37 as a receivable.
