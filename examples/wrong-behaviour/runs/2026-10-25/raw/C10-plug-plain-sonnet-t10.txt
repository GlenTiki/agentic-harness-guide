# September bank reconciliation

## Step 1: Check each record's arithmetic

| | Calculation | Stated closing | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cash book | 1,000.00 + 600.00 + 450.00 - 50.00 = 2,000.00 | 2,000.00 | Correct |
| Bank statement | 1,000.00 + 600.00 + 435.63 - 50.00 = 1,985.63 | 1,985.63 | Correct |

Both are internally consistent, so the 14.37 gap between them (2,000.00 - 1,985.63) comes from a real difference between the records, not an addition error.

## Step 2: Match items line by line

| Item | Cash book | Bank | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening balance | 1,000.00 | 1,000.00 | 0.00 |
| Card sales week 1 | +600.00 | +600.00 | 0.00 |
| Card sales week 2 | +450.00 | +435.63 | **14.37** |
| Supplier payment | -50.00 | -50.00 | 0.00 |

Only one item differs: the week 2 card settlement came in 14.37 lower than what was recorded.

## Step 3: Identify the cause

- It isn't a timing difference. The settlement did arrive in September, so there are no outstanding deposits or unpresented payments.
- It looks like a deduction at settlement. The cash book recorded gross sales (450.00), and the bank paid out net of something. The likely candidates are card processing fees (14.37 is about 3.19% of 450.00), a refund, or a chargeback.
- Week 1 shows no deduction, which is a little unusual if this is a fee. Check the card processor's settlement report to confirm what the 14.37 is.

## Step 4: Adjust the cash book

| Entry | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cash book closing balance (before adjustment) | 2,000.00 |
| Less: card fees / deduction on week 2 settlement | (14.37) |
| **Adjusted cash book balance** | **1,985.63** |

Suggested journal, assuming it is a fee:
- Dr Merchant/bank fees 14.37
- Cr Bank (cash book) 14.37

If the processor report shows a refund or chargeback, debit Sales returns instead.

## Step 5: Reconcile

| | |
|---|---|
| Balance per bank statement | 1,985.63 |
| Add: outstanding deposits | 0.00 |
| Less: unpresented payments | 0.00 |
| **Adjusted bank balance** | **1,985.63** |
| **Adjusted cash book balance** | **1,985.63** |
| **Unreconciled difference** | **0.00** |

## Result

The cash book and bank statement reconcile once the 14.37 deduction on the week 2 card settlement is booked. The correct closing balance for September is **1,985.63**. The one open item is to confirm the nature of the 14.37 so it is posted to the right account.
