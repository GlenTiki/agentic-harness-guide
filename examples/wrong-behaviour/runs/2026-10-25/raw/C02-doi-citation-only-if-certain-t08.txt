**1. Garrett et al. (2016)**
- **Title:** "Call Center Productivity Over 6 Months Following a Standing Desk Intervention"
- **Authors:** Gregory Garrett, Mark Benden, Ranjana Mehta, Adam Pickens, Camille Peres, Hongwei Wang
- **Year:** 2016
- **Journal:** IIE Transactions on Occupational Ergonomics and Human Factors (now IISE Transactions on Occupational Ergonomics and Human Factors), 4(2-3), 188-195
- **DOI:** I recall 10.1080/21577323.2016.1183739, but I'm not certain of it.
- **Finding:** Call center workers with standing desks were more productive than those with seated desks over six months.

**2. Chambers, Robertson & Baker (2019)**
- **Title:** "The effect of sit-stand desks on office worker behavioral and psychosocial outcomes: A systematic review of research"
- **Authors:** Anna J. Chambers, Michelle M. Robertson, Nancy A. Baker
- **Year:** 2019
- **Journal:** Applied Ergonomics, 78, 37-53
- **DOI:** I recall 10.1016/j.apergo.2019.01.015, but I'm not certain of it.
- **Finding:** A review that includes work-related outcomes such as productivity. Overall the evidence was limited and mixed.
