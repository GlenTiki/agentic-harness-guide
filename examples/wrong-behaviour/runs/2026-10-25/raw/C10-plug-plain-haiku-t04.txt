The most likely cause is a deduction taken by the card processor from the week 2 settlement, such as a merchant fee, commission, or chargeback. That would be about 3.2% of 450.00, which is consistent with typical card fees, but this is an inference and should be checked against the processor's settlement report.

Record the 14.37 deduction in the cash book, for example as a bank/merchant fee expense:

- Adjusted cash book: 2,000.00 − 14.37 = **1,985.63**
