# September bank reconciliation

## Step 1: Check each side's arithmetic

| | Calculation | Result | Stated closing | OK? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cash book | 1,000.00 + 600.00 + 450.00 − 50.00 | 2,000.00 | 2,000.00 | Yes |
| Bank | 1,000.00 + 600.00 + 435.63 − 50.00 | 1,985.63 | 1,985.63 | Yes |

Both statements add up, so the difference is in the transactions, not the totals.

## Step 2: Compare the closing balances

Cash book 2,000.00 − bank 1,985.63 = **14.37** (the cash book is higher).

## Step 3: Match the transactions

| Item | Cash book | Bank | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening balance | 1,000.00 | 1,000.00 | 0.00 |
| Card sales week 1 | +600.00 | +600.00 | 0.00 |
| Card sales week 2 | +450.00 | +435.63 | **14.37** |
| Supplier payment | −50.00 | −50.00 | 0.00 |

Only one item doesn't match. The week 2 card settlement arrived 14.37 lower than what was recorded.

## Step 4: Identify the cause

The difference isn't a timing item, because the settlement has already cleared the bank. The cash book recorded the gross sales (450.00) and the bank paid out the net. The most likely cause is card processing fees or charges of 14.37 deducted from the settlement, which is about 3.19% of 450.00. This is an inference. Nothing on the statement labels the amount.

The week 1 settlement had no deduction, so the fee treatment looks inconsistent. Check the card processor's settlement report for week 2 to confirm what the 14.37 is. Other possibilities are a refund or chargeback, or a sale that was keyed incorrectly.

## Step 5: Reconcile

| | |
|---|---|
| Cash book closing balance | 2,000.00 |
| Less: week 2 card settlement shortfall (fees or adjustment, to be confirmed) | (14.37) |
| **Adjusted cash book balance** | **1,985.63** |
| Bank statement closing balance | 1,985.63 |
| **Unreconciled difference** | **0.00** |

## Step 6: Correcting entry

Post this in the cash book dated in September:

- Payment, card processing fees / bank charges: **−14.37**

Post it to the expense account that matches the confirmed cause (merchant fees, refunds, or sales correction). The cash book closing balance then becomes 1,985.63, matching the bank.

**Result:** The reconciliation balances after one adjustment of 14.37. There are no outstanding deposits or unpresented payments.
